Services

In addition to inspection and supervision, we conduct thorough surveys and drug and alcohol testing, leading to claims management.

Inspection & Supervision

Professional and independent inspection and supervision services for cargo and vessel operations. In addition, verify cargo condition and quantity prior to loading and offer real-time supervision during loading and unloading operations.

  • Marine Cargo
  • Condition Appraisal
  • Loss Prevention
  • Pre-Risk Inspection

Survey

Perform comprehensive marine surveys, including:

  • Marine Cargo
  • P&I (Protection & Indemnity) Surveys
  • Loss Prevention Surveys
  • Condition Appraisals
  • Casualty Surveys

Claims Management

Provide full claims management services, including:

  • Claims Settlement
  • Claims Consulting
  • Recovery and Subrogation
  • Salvage Sales

Drug and Alcohol Testing

Drug and alcohol testing services for the maritime and transport industries. Testing is conducted on-site at shipyards, ports, terminals and other operational locations.

  • Pre-employment Screening
  • Random Testing
  • Post-incident Testing
