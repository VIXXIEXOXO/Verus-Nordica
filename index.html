Services

Experienced professionals offer a wide range of average and survey agency services.

Inspection and Supervision
Professional and independent inspection and supervision services for cargo and vessel operations. In addition, verify cargo condition and quantity prior to loading and offer real-time supervision during loading and unloading operations.
Survey
Perform comprehensive marine surveys, including: Marine Cargo Surveys, P&I (Protection & Indemnity) Surveys, H&M (Hull & Machinery) Surveys, Loss Prevention Surveys, Condition Appraisals, Casualty Surveys.
Claims Management
Provide full claims management services, including: Claims Settlement, Claims Consulting, Recovery and Subrogation, Salvage Sales.
